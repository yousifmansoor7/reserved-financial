Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There's no fee to join Reserved Financial. Your only costs are your state licensing course and exam, and we'll point you to affordable options.

Is this a course or a program I buy?

No. This is a real career with a licensed agency. You get live training, hands-on mentorship and real clients, not a set of videos.

Do I need sales experience?

No. Many of our agents had never sold before. We teach you the products, the conversations and the process from the ground up.

Can I start part-time?

Yes. Plenty of agents start part-time alongside their current job and move to full-time once their income supports it.

How long does getting licensed take?

Most people finish their pre-licensing course and pass the state exam in about 1 to 3 weeks, depending on their state and schedule. We help you every step of the way.

Will I have a mentor?

Yes. You'll be paired with an experienced producer who helps with your first calls, your first applications and your growth after that.

How much can I make?

It depends on your effort and consistency. You're paid on commission, so there's no ceiling, but income is not guaranteed. We'll walk you through real compensation details on your interview.

Do I have to find my own clients?

You won't be starting from scratch. We show you proven ways to generate leads and give you the systems to follow up, so you always know who to call next.
